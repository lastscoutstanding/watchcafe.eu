How accurate is your watch, really?

Free browser tools to measure and track your mechanical watches. No app, no account, no timegrapher needed. Just your phone and a few minutes a day.

s/day

Seconds per day, fast or slow. Compare your watch with an exact reference clock for a few days and see its rate, with charts and a margin of error.

The tools

Rate Meter

How many seconds a day does it gain or lose? Per position, and compared with COSC and other standards.

Power Reserve

How long does it run on a full wind? Read the stop time straight off the hands.

Collection

All your watches in one place: details, service log, what you wore today and a printable report.

Moon Phase

Set your moonphase complication right: how many presses, with the real new and full moons.

How it works

1Open watchcafe.eu

On your phone or laptop. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

2Match your watch

Set the on-screen clock to what your watch shows and tap Record.

3Repeat, then read

Again over a few days. The Rate Meter works out the rate.
